Welcome to Flower Valley

Helping Children Reach Their Full Potential

Flower Valley Occupational Therapy Centre is a pediatric occupational therapy centre dedicated to helping children develop the skills they need for everyday life, learning and social participation.

Our goal is to identify challenges early and provide personalised therapy in a supportive, caring and child-friendly environment.

Step-by-Step Journey

How Therapy Works

A clear, structured 4-step pathway designed for your child's success.

01

Initial Consultation

Understand the child's concerns, developmental history, and daily functional needs through a friendly conversation with parents.

02

Assessment

Conduct standardized clinical assessments to identify specific areas where the child needs support (motor, sensory, focus, social).

03

Personalised Therapy Plan

Develop targeted activity programs customized to the child's unique developmental level, interests, and goals.

04

Progress Monitoring

Regularly evaluate progress, adjust therapy goals, and provide empowering guidance for parents and teachers.
