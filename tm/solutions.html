Energy Optimization

HVAC Retrofitting & Plant Room Optimization

Aging HVAC systems consume up to 40% more electricity while suffering frequent downtime. ThinkMech retrofits legacy plant rooms with high-efficiency chillers, VFD automation, and smart PLC controllers without interrupting building operations.

Core Retrofitting Services:

Chiller Replacement
Compressor Overhaul
Heat Exchanger Upgrades
PLC & BMS Integration
Cooling Tower Retrofit
VFD Drives Installation
Plant Room Piping Optimization
Refrigerant Retrofitting

Retrofit Key Benefits:

  • Reduce Energy Costs: Up to 35% reduction in KW/TR.
  • Increase Equipment Life: Extend chiller lifespan by 7-10 years.
  • Improve Thermal Performance: Rapid cooling & precise temperature hold.
  • Lower Maintenance Costs: Fewer breakdowns and routine repairs.

Air Handling Benefits:

  • Better Airflow Distribution: Balanced static pressure across all zones.
  • Less Acoustic Noise: Quiet EC fan technology & acoustic lining.
  • Energy Saving Motors: Ultra-efficient IE4/EC motor replacements.
  • Improved IAQ: Multi-tier HEPA & MERV filter integration.
