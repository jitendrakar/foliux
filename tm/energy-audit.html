Regulatory Compliance

HVAC Qualification Standards (DQ, IQ, OQ, PQ)

Rigorous validation documentation adhering to US FDA, WHO GMP, and ISO clean room compliance protocols.

DQ

Design Qualification

Verifying psychrometric calculations, CAD layouts, material compliance, and air change rate specifications before fabrication.

IQ

Installation Qualification

Auditing physical installation, duct seal integrity, HEPA filter integrity test (PAO/DOP), electrical wiring, and sensor placements.

OQ

Operation Qualification

Testing airflow velocity, room pressure differential, temperature/RH control loops, and alarm limits under static conditions.

PQ

Performance Qualification

Validating room air particle counts, thermal stability, and recovery time under full operating heat & personnel load.
