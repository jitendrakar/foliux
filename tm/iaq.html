UVGI Germicidal Irradiation

C-band UV lamps installed in AHUs destroying 99.9% of airborne viruses, bacteria, and fungal mold spores on cooling coils.

HEPA H14 & ULPA Filtration

Medical grade absolute filters arresting 99.995% of micro-particles down to 0.12 microns for operating theaters and ISO clean rooms.

Precision Humidity Control

Desiccant dehumidifiers and steam humidifiers holding RH strictly within 45%-55% to prevent bacterial growth.

Plasma ionizers neutralizing volatile organic compounds (VOCs), odor molecules, and static charges across building ductwork.

Gas Phase Filtration

Activated carbon and potassium permanganate beds absorbing hazardous gases, chemical fumes, and diesel exhaust.

Pressure Monitoring

Positive pressure cascades for sterile clean rooms and negative pressure isolation chambers for infection control.

Ozonisation Systems

Controlled ozone generators for organic odor breakdown in commercial kitchens and central return air plenums.

Standalone Air Purifiers
